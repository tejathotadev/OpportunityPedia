# OpportunityPedia — Quick Start Guide

Welcome. This short guide explains **what OpportunityPedia is**, **why it helps you**, and **how to use it** after you sign in.

---

## What is OpportunityPedia?

**OpportunityPedia** is an opportunity intelligence platform for staffing and business development teams.

It brings hiring signals and US government contract opportunities into **one place**, so your team can spot hot leads faster and act on them together.

---

## The problem we solve

Finding good opportunities today usually means:

- Checking many job boards one by one  
- Searching government sites manually  
- Sharing leads in chats or spreadsheets  
- Losing track of who followed up  

That is slow, easy to miss, and hard for a team to manage.

---

## What you achieve with us

With OpportunityPedia you can:

1. **Scan** many sources in one click (Radar)  
2. **See** commercial hiring and government opportunities clearly  
3. **Focus** on the hottest signals first  
4. **Assign** leads and track outreach as a team  
5. **Unlock** handpicked vendor / shared opportunities after a successful scan  

Less hunting. More closing.

## Simple map of the product

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Radar** | The scanner. It looks across job boards and government sources, then refreshes your data. |
| **Overview** | Your home dashboard — totals, hot signals, and the place to click **Run**. |

## Your first session (step by step)

### Step 1 — Sign in

Go to the login page and sign in with your account.

You land on **Overview**.

### Step 2 — Run Radar

On **Overview**, click **Run**.

Radar starts scanning. Wait until it finishes (you’ll see status updates).

> Tip: Radar may need a short wait between runs. If **Run** is unavailable, check the message or open **Radar runs**.

### Step 3 — Check Overview

After a successful run, Overview shows your latest picture:

- How many opportunities you have  
- What looks **Very Hot** or **Hot**  
- Filters like **All / Government / Commercial**

Use this page to decide where to dig first.

### Step 4 — Explore Opportunities

Open **Opportunities** in the left sidebar.

Here you browse what Radar discovered:

- **Commercial** — companies that look active in hiring  
- **Government** — tenders and procurement-style signals  

Click any row to open details. From there you can review, assign, or start outreach.

### Step 5 — Check Vendors

Open **Vendors**.

These are curated opportunities shared with your workspace. They appear after a **successful Radar run**.

Filter by **All / Commercial / Government** if you want a narrower view.

### Step 6 — Work as a team

- Open an opportunity → assign it if needed  
- Use **My Assignments** for your personal queue  
- Use **Team Activity** to see team follow-ups  
- Use outreach (including AI draft, when available) to send a professional message  

> **Tip:** Workspace owners should open **Settings → Email** and add company SMTP so outreach leaves from your business mailbox (not the platform).

### Step 7 — Stay on top of updates

- Check notifications for new findings  
- Use search at the top when you know a company or keyword  
- Re-run Radar when you want a fresh scan (respect any cooldown)

## Quick meanings (no jargon)

**Hot / Very Hot** — How strong or urgent the signal looks. Very Hot usually deserves attention first.

**Commercial** — Private-sector / company hiring style opportunities.

**Government** — Public-sector / contract-style opportunities.

**Radar run** — One full scan from start to finish.

**Vendors (in this app)** — Shared, handpicked opportunities for your workspace — not a separate marketplace to manage vendors yourself.

---

## Suggested daily rhythm

1. Open **Overview**  
2. Click **Run** (when available)  
3. Scan **Opportunities** for new or hot items  
4. Check **Vendors** for shared leads  
5. Work your **My Assignments**  
6. Glance at **Team Activity** so nothing slips  

That’s it.
