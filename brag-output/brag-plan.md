# Brag Plan: OpportunityPedia

## What is this app?
OpportunityPedia (by OpportunityX) is an opportunity intelligence product that helps teams find hiring signals and government contracts in one place — then prioritize and act.

## The angle
A calm, premium product film: opportunity is fragmented; Radar makes it visible; teams act before it disappears. No joke energy — confidence through restraint.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed warm paper field. One line at scale: **We make opportunity easier to see.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- Fragmented discovery collapses into one Radar **Run**
- Opportunity cards with **Very Hot** / commercial + government
- Discover → Prioritize → Act loop

## Outro / punchline
Forest green field. **OpportunityPedia** + tagline. Soft hold.

## User flow worth showing
Login world → **Run Radar** on Overview → **Opportunities / Vendors** appear with hot signals → team can act.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for B2B opportunity intelligence
- Interpretation: 4 scenes, slow crossfades, mixed-case type, one idea per beat, voiceover complements (does not read) on-screen copy

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: ~22–24 seconds (flex to voiceover)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f7f7f2` (paper)
- Accent: `#12372a` (forest) + `#21805d` (signal)
- Text: `#111827` (ink) / `#475569` (graphite)
- Display font: Instrument Serif (fallback Georgia)
- Body font: Inter (fallback system-ui)
- Strongest visual element: editorial hero + forest CTA + Very Hot temperature signal

## Share copy (draft)
Opportunity is already out there. OpportunityPedia helps you see it — scan, spot what’s hot, and act.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents under a warm corporate bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (business / polished)
- Music treatment: start near 0, volume ~0.14 under VO, slight fade under final logo
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; optional locks near 5.19 / 11.47 / 18.55 for scene lands
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; forest glow / card presence breathes with RMS — no waveform visuals
- SFX posture: sparse; soft drop / switch on card and stage reveals
- Audio-coupled moments: Discover/Prioritize/Act sequential; final logo land
- Restraint rule: no chaotic hits; polish over density

## Voiceover script
Opportunity is fragmented across dozens of sites.
OpportunityPedia brings hiring signals and government contracts into one place.
Run Radar. Spot what's very hot. Assign, outreach, and act.
OpportunityPedia — we make opportunity easier to see.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 5.0s
Warm paper `#f7f7f2` with subtle field grid. Label OPPORTUNITYX. Hero line: We make opportunity easier to see.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: VO opens the problem framing; music soft bed
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: vol-10 bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Radar — 6.0s
UI-inspired Overview panel on paper. Primary action **Run**. Short line: Scan job boards + SAM.gov in one click.
Sequential/interaction: cursor/pulse on Run button
Audio intent: VO explains Radar; soft click/drop on Run
Audio-coupled idea: simulated tap on Run
Music: continue
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Signals — 7.0s
Three product beats arrive: Discover / Prioritize / Act. Mini opportunity cards with Very Hot badge and Commercial / Government labels.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three stages one by one, then cards
Audio intent: VO on acting; gentle sequential SFX
Audio-coupled idea: beat-grid stage reveals
Music: continue
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 5.0s
Forest `#12372a` field with light grid. OpportunityPedia wordmark. Tagline in muted white. CTA feel: Explore OpportunityPedia.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: VO closes on tagline; music fades; soft logo hit
Audio-coupled idea: final logo
Music: fade under logo
Transition mood: hold / end
